Pulling back the walls of how AI thinks — for medicine that can trust it.

MAX Lab studies how thinking models reason, and turns that understanding into diagnostic tools clinicians can question, verify, and rely on.

Explore our research Join the lab
What we do

Four questions guide every project

We sit at the intersection of neurology, diagnostic medicine, and artificial intelligence — working from mechanism outward to application.

01

Model reasoning

Probing how thinking models reach conclusions — not just what they conclude.

02

Diagnostic precision

Turning that understanding into sharper, more accurate AI-assisted diagnosis.

03

Precision medicine

Adapting model behavior to the individual patient, not the statistical average.

04

Safety & reliability

Stress-testing systems before they ever reach a clinical setting.

Why it matters

A black box isn't good enough for a diagnosis.

Medicine advances by understanding mechanisms, not just outcomes. We hold AI in healthcare to the same standard — open enough to question, rigorous enough to trust.

Who we're looking for

We welcome candidates from medicine, computer science, data science, and related fields. What matters most is curiosity about the reasoning behind AI systems and a commitment to rigorous, honest science. Prior AI/ML experience helps but isn't always required — strong clinical reasoning translates well.
